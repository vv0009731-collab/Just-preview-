About JustPreview

JustPreview is a free movie discovery tool. Instead of scrolling through five different apps to figure out what to watch, JustPreview brings trending movies, current theatrical releases, trailers, cast details, and streaming availability into one simple page.

What you can do here

  • Browse what's trending this week and what's currently playing in theatres
  • Search for any movie by title
  • Watch trailers without leaving the page
  • See cast, rating, genres, and a synopsis for any title
  • Check where a movie is available to stream, rent, or buy
  • Save titles to a personal watchlist to come back to later

Where the data comes from

Movie details, posters, and trailers are powered by The Movie Database (TMDB). Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Why it exists

JustPreview was built to answer one simple question fast: "What is this movie, and where can I actually watch it?" — without ads-heavy piracy sites or ten tabs of separate streaming apps.
