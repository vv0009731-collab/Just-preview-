Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

JustPreview ("we", "us") is a movie discovery site. This page explains what data is collected when you use it and how it's used.

What we don't collect

JustPreview does not require an account, login, email address, or phone number. We do not knowingly collect personal information from you.

Watchlist

The "My watchlist" feature saves the movie IDs you add to a list on our server so they're available when you come back. This list is not tied to your name, email, or any personal identifier — it's just stored to make the feature work.

Third-party data sources

Movie details, posters, ratings, and trailers are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability ("Where to watch") is provided by JustWatch. When you click a streaming link, you leave JustPreview and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies and advertising

JustPreview may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use basic, aggregated analytics (such as page views) to understand how the site is used. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

JustPreview is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
